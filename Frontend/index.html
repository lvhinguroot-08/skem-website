Who We Are

About S.K Engineering and Manufacturing — Industrial Heritage in Mahesana

S.K Engineering and Manufacturing (SKEM) is an established industrial engineering and fabrication enterprise based in Mahesana, Gujarat.

We specialize in metal fabrication, heavy structural erection, pipeline spooling, and industrial equipment manufacturing. Whether fabricating high-elevation lattice flare stacks for oil and gas installations, mobile frac tanks for energy operations, or complex steel frameworks for commercial leisure developments, our work reflects meticulous attention to design tolerances, weld integrity, and worksite safety.

Heavy Fabrication Yard

Equipped workshop and outdoor yard for beam welding, cutting, and assembly in Mahesana.

Certified Business

Custom Manufacturing

Fabrication of custom skids, storage tanks, and vehicle-mounted field units.

Direct Leadership

What We Do

Industrial Solutions Tailored for Critical Operations

From design calculation and shop-floor fabrication to field assembly and equipment commissioning, explore SKEM's four specialized service divisions.

Division 01

ENGINEERING

Practical engineering and worksite layout solutions designed for real-world functionality. We review client specifications, coordinate structural dimensions, plan piping routes, and ensure every fabricated assembly aligns perfectly with operational equipment.

  • Piping spool layout & joint angle calculations
  • Process skid integration & nozzle orientation
  • Sensor, valve, & instrumentation positioning
  • Worksite assembly sequencing & lifting planning
Discuss Engineering Work
Division 02

FABRICATION

High-capacity steel fabrication carried out by experienced welders and fitters in our Mahesana facility. We cut, bevel, fit, weld, and grind heavy plates, I-beams, channels, and seamless pipe spools to exacting industrial standards.

  • Heavy I-beam & channel column fabrication
  • High-pressure piping manifolds & valve headers
  • Welded structural trusses, base plates & brackets
  • Precision joint beveling, grinding & weld finishing
Discuss Fabrication Work
Division 03

MANUFACTURING

Production of heavy industrial machinery units, specialized liquid tanks, and custom skids. From mobile frac tanks for oilfield service companies to truck-mounted pumping units and process heaters, we manufacture durable equipment designed for harsh environments.

  • Industrial liquid & mobile frac storage tanks (e.g. 52 M³)
  • Truck-mounted fluid pumping & tool skids
  • Industrial process vessels, separators & heaters
  • Mobile bunk houses & rugged worksite cabins
Discuss Manufacturing Work
Division 04

STRUCTURES

Engineering, shop fabrication, and on-site erection of tall steel structures, industrial pipe trestles, plant shed frames, and architectural frameworks. We handle multi-section vertical assemblies that require certified lifting and crane placement.

  • High-elevation lattice flare stacks & towers
  • Overhead pipeline trestles & gantry racks
  • Industrial shed structures, canopies & crane rails
  • Specialized recreation & waterpark structural towers

Why Businesses Partner with SKEM

We operate with straightforward engineering principles: high technical precision, robust fabrication in our yard, and hands-on site execution.

Engineering-Focused Approach

Every job begins with careful assessment of structural loads, weld requirements, and dimensional constraints so that parts assemble cleanly on site without expensive delays.

Dedicated Fabrication Yard

Our Mahesana fabrication facility features spacious indoor sheds and outdoor layout yards equipped with heavy welding transformers, cutting bays, and structural assembly jigs.

Real Worksite Execution

We don't just supply metal; our team goes to work on live operational facilities, oilfields, and commercial sites to erect, align, and connect completed equipment safely.

Rigorous Weld & Material Quality

From bevel preparation to full-penetration welding on pressure lines, our fabricators maintain uniform penetration, clean bead profiles, and robust weld strength.

Direct Communication

No layered corporate bureaucracy. You speak directly with our key decision-maker Sohil Khan for technical queries, progress updates, and transparent quotation.
